# IT Survival Guide

Welcome to the IT Survival Guide. This is a comprehensive resource designed for absolute beginners with zero technical background. It assumes you may type with two fingers, struggle with technical English, and have never written code.

## Critical Warning: The English Bottleneck

> [!WARNING] Essential Reading Before Starting
> **The IT industry operates primarily in English.** Approximately 90% of documentation, error messages, forums, and advanced resources are in English. If English is not your first language, this will become your primary bottleneck.
> 
> Do not skip the [[00-English-Support|English Support]] section. Use the provided AI prompts to translate and simplify concepts as you go.

## How to Use This Guide

This guide follows a "Fail Forward" philosophy. You are free to explore any section, but be aware that skipping fundamentals often leads to frustration.

1.  **Start Here**: Read [[00-English-Support]] then go to [[00-MOC-Basics]].
2.  **Learn Fundamentals**: Master the basics in `00-BASICS`.
3.  **Explore Prerequisites**: Move to `00-PREREQUISITES` for core tech concepts.
4.  **Choose a Path**: Visit [[00-MOC-Paths]] to select a career track.
5.  **Use Tools**: Leverage `00-PROMPTS` for AI help and `99-Exports/Anki` for flashcards.
