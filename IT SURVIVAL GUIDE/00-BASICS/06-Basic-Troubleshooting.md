# Basic Troubleshooting

## The IT Mindset
Things will break. This is normal. Professional IT workers spend 50% of their time fixing broken things. The difference is they have a systematic approach.

## Step 1: The Universal Fix
**Have you tried turning it off and on again?**
- Restart your computer.
- Restart your router/modem.
- Close and reopen the application.
This fixes approximately 40% of all common issues by clearing temporary memory glitches.

## Step 2: Identify the Change
Ask yourself: "What changed right before this broke?"
- Did I install new software?
- Did I update Windows/macOS?
- Did I click a strange link?
- Did I move a file?

If you know what changed, you can often reverse it (uninstall, undo update, restore file).

## Step 3: Check Connections
- Is the power cable plugged in firmly?
- Is the Ethernet cable clicked into place?
- Is Wi-Fi actually connected?
- Are peripherals (mouse/keyboard) paired or plugged in?

## Step 4: Read the Error Message
Do not ignore pop-ups.
- Take a screenshot.
- Copy the text.
- Search it using [[04-Google-Fu|Search Skills]].

## Step 5: Isolate the Problem
- Does this happen in another browser?
- Does this happen with another user account?
- Does this happen on another device?
If it only happens in one specific case, the problem is local to that case.

> [!TIP] Document Everything
> Keep a simple text file called `issues.log`. Write down what broke and how you fixed it. This becomes your personal knowledge base.

## Micro-Challenge
Next time something minor breaks (printer, app crash, wifi), do not ask for help immediately. Follow these 5 steps first. Only then seek assistance.
