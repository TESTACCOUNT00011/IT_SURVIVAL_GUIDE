# 03 - Typing: Yes, It Matters (But Don't Stress!)

## 🎯 The Truth About Typing

**Bad news:** If you hunt-and-peck (type with two fingers), you'll be slow and frustrated.

**Good news:** You don't need to be perfect. You just need to be *better than you are now*.

---

## 🤔 Why Should I Care?

Imagine:
- **Hunt-and-peck:** 20 words per minute (wpm)
- **Basic touch typing:** 40 wpm
- **Comfortable typist:** 60+ wpm

**That's 3x faster!** Over a year, that's HUNDREDS of hours saved.

Plus:
- Less frustration when coding or writing
- You can think while typing (instead of thinking about where the keys are)
- Looks more professional

---

## 🖐️ What Is "Touch Typing"?

Touch typing = typing without looking at the keyboard.

**Home Row Position:**
```
Left hand:  A S D F
Right hand: J K L ;
Thumbs:     Spacebar
```

Your fingers should rest on these keys. Feel the little bumps on **F** and **J**? Those are there so you can find home without looking!

---

## 📝 Which Finger Hits Which Key?

```
        [Q] [W] [E] [R] [T]   [Y] [U] [I] [O] [P]
         [A] [S] [D] [F] [G]   [H] [J] [K] [L] [;]
          [Z] [X] [C] [V] [B]   [N] [M] [,] [.] [/]
           ↑                       ↑
          Pinky                  Pinky
```

**Left Hand:**
- Pinky: Q, A, Z
- Ring: W, S, X
- Middle: E, D, C
- Index: R, F, T, G, V, B

**Right Hand:**
- Index: Y, H, N, U, J, M
- Middle: I, K, ,
- Ring: O, L, .
- Pinky: P, ;, /, ?

**Thumbs:** Spacebar (either thumb works)

## 🏋️ How to Practice (Without Going Crazy)

### Week 1-2: Learn the Home Row
- Just practice A S D F J K L ;
- Go SLOW. Accuracy > Speed
- 10-15 minutes per day

### Week 3-4: Add Top and Bottom Rows
- Practice full rows: QWERTY... then ASDF... then ZXCV...
- Still go slow!
- 15-20 minutes per day

### Week 5+: Full Practice
- Type actual words and sentences
- Start paying attention to speed
- 20-30 minutes per day

## 💡 Tips for Success

### ✅ DO:
- Sit up straight
- Keep wrists slightly elevated (not resting on the desk)
- Look at the SCREEN, not your hands
- Go SLOW at first (speed comes naturally)
- Practice every day (even 10 minutes helps)
- Take breaks when frustrated

### ❌ DON'T:
- Look at your hands (cover them with a towel if needed!)
- Rush (you'll develop bad habits)
- Practice for hours (you'll burn out)
- Get discouraged (progress is slow but real)

## 📊 What's a Good Speed?

| Level | Words Per Minute | Who You Are |
|-------|------------------|-------------|
| 10-20 wpm | Beginner | You're starting here - that's okay! |
| 30-40 wpm | Average | Most people end up here |
| 50-60 wpm | Good | You're faster than most! |
| 70-80 wpm | Great | Solid typing skills |
| 90+ wpm | Wow | Do you do this for a living? |

**Realistic goal:** Aim for 40-50 wpm within 2-3 months of practice.

---

## 😤 What If I'm Still Bad After Practicing?

**That's fine!** Not everyone becomes a touch typing master.

As long as you can:
- Type without constant frustration
- Hit the right keys most of the time
- Go at a reasonable pace (30+ wpm)

You're good enough for most tech jobs. Coding isn't about typing fast—it's about thinking clearly.
