# Typing Skills

If you hunt-and-peck with two fingers, you will struggle. Coding requires lots of typing.

## Why It Matters

- **Flow**: Slow typing breaks your concentration.
- **Syntax**: Code uses special characters (`{ } [ ] ; < >`) that are hard to find slowly.
- **Fatigue**: Looking at the keyboard causes neck strain.

## The Goal

- **Target**: 40-50 Words Per Minute (WPM) with touch typing (no looking).
- **Accuracy**: More important than speed. Errors cause bugs.

## How to Learn

1. **Home Row**: Place fingers on `ASDF` (left) and `JKL;` (right).
2. **No Peeking**: Cover your hands with a towel if needed.
3. **Practice Daily**: 15 minutes a day is better than 2 hours once a week.

## Recommended Tools

- **Keybr.com**: Generates nonsense words to force muscle memory.
- **TypingClub.com**: Structured lessons for beginners.
- **Monkeytype**: For testing speed once you know the keys.

> [!TIP] Special Characters
> Programmers need to type symbols quickly. Practice lines like:
> `const x = { key: "value", arr: [1, 2, 3] };`
