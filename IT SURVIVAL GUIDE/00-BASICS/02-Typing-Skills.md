# Typing Skills

## Why This Matters
In IT, you will type all day. Hunt-and-peck (two-finger) typing causes fatigue and slows your thinking. Proper touch typing allows you to focus on logic rather than finding keys.

## The Home Row Position
Place your fingers on the middle row of letters:
- **Left Hand**: Pinky on `A`, Ring on `S`, Middle on `D`, Index on `F`.
- **Right Hand**: Index on `J`, Middle on `K`, Ring on `L`, Pinky on `;`.
- **Thumbs**: Both on the `Spacebar`.

Notice the small bumps on the `F` and `J` keys? Those are tactile guides so you don't need to look down.

## Rules for Learning
1. **Never Look Down**: Cover your hands with a towel if necessary.
2. **Accuracy Over Speed**: Speed comes naturally. Do not rush.
3. **Use All Fingers**: Each finger has specific keys it controls.

## Recommended Tools
- **TypingClub.com**: Free, gamified lessons for absolute beginners.
- **Keybr.com**: Uses AI to generate words based on your weak keys.
- **Monkeytype**: Minimalist practice for when you improve.

## Micro-Challenge
- Spend 15 minutes daily on [[TypingClub]](https://www.typingclub.com) or Keybr.
- Goal: Reach 30 Words Per Minute (WPM) with 95% accuracy before moving to coding.

> [!WARNING] The Plateau
> You will feel slower at first as you unlearn bad habits. This is normal. Push through the first week.
