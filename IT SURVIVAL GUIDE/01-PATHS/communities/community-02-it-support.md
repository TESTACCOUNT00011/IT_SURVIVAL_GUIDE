# IT Support Communities

## Top Tier Communities

### Reddit
- **r/techsupport**: Help users solve problems (great for practice).
- **r/sysadmin**: War stories and advice from system administrators.
- **r/CompTIA**: Discussion about A+, Network+, Security+ exams.
- **r/HelpDesk**: Specific to help desk professionals.

### Discord Servers
- **Tech Support Server**: General tech help community.
- **Professor Messer Discord**: Active A+ and Network+ study groups.
- **Homelab**: For building your own server labs.

### Specialized Forums
- **Spiceworks Community**: Professional IT forum, very active.
- **ServerFault**: Q&A for professional sysadmins (higher bar than StackOverflow).
- **Microsoft Tech Community**: Official Microsoft forums.

## Practice Platforms
- **TryHackMe Pre-Security**: Safe environment to learn security basics.
- **Cisco Packet Tracer**: Network simulation software.
- **VirtualBox/VMware**: Build your own virtual lab at home.

## Events & Meetups
- **Local ISSA Chapters**: Information Systems Security Association meetups.
- **BSides Conferences**: Community-run security events (often free/cheap).
- **Meetup.com**: Search "IT Professionals" in your area.

> [!TIP] Building Experience
> If you have no job experience, build a homelab. Document it on a blog. Join r/homelab and share your projects. This counts as practical experience in interviews.
