# IT Support Communities

Connect with sysadmins and help desk professionals.

## Top Tier Communities

- **r/sysadmin (Reddit)**
  - War stories, advice, and venting.
  - Strict no-homework policy.
  - Great for career reality checks.

- **Spiceworks Community**
  - Forum for IT pros.
  - Hardware and software recommendations.
  - Vendor-neutral advice.

- **Professor Messer Discord**
  - Focused on CompTIA certifications.
  - Study groups and exam tips.

## Specialized Chats

- **Homelab**: For building personal servers (Reddit/Discord).
- **Network Engineering**: Deep dive into routing/switching.
- **PowerShell**: Scripting help and sharing.

## Practice Platforms

- **TryHackMe**: "Pre-Security" and "Complete Beginner" paths.
- **Cisco Packet Tracer**: Build virtual networks.
- **VirtualBox**: Create your own sandbox environment.

## How to Get Value

1. **Document Your Issues**: Keep a log of problems you solved.
2. **Share Solutions**: Post fixes you discover on forums.
3. **Build a Homelab**: Practice breaking and fixing things safely.
4. **Certify**: Use communities to study for A+, Network+, Security+.

> [!WARNING] Professionalism
> Do not share sensitive company data or screenshots of internal systems. Anonymize everything.
