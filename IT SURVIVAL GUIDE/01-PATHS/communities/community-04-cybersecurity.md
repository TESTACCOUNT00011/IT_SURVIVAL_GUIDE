# 🔒 Cybersecurity Communities

The security community is unique: it values skills over degrees, but it demands ethics and discretion. "Trust but verify" is the motto.

## 🏆 Top Tier Communities (Start Here)

### 1. **TryHackMe Discord**
- **Link**: [discord.gg/tryhackme](https://discord.gg/tryhackme)
- **Vibe**: Beginner-friendly, gamified learning.
- **Best For**: Absolute beginners, CTF walkthroughs, room hints.
- **Activity**: Very High (24/7).

### 2. **r/cybersecurity & r/netsec (Reddit)**
- **Links**: [reddit.com/r/cybersecurity](https://reddit.com/r/cybersecurity) | [reddit.com/r/netsec](https://reddit.com/r/netsec)
- **Vibe**: News-heavy, professional discussion.
- **Best For**: Latest breaches, vulnerability disclosures, career advice.
- **Note**: r/netsec is more technical; r/cybersecurity is broader.

### 3. **HackTheBox Discord**
- **Link**: [discord.gg/hackthebox](https://discord.gg/hackthebox)
- **Vibe**: Competitive, advanced users.
- **Best For**: Hard machine walkthroughs, pension challenges, networking with pros.
- **Warning**: Can be intimidating for total newbies; start with TryHackMe first.

## 🛠️ Practice Platforms (Gamified Learning)
- **OverTheWire**: Command line and security wargames (Start with "Bandit").
- **PicoCTF**: Excellent for beginners and students.
- **RangeForce**: Interactive cyber ranges (some free modules).
- **LetsDefend**: Blue team simulation (incident response).

## 📅 Conferences & Events
- **DEF CON**: The biggest hacker conference (held in Vegas, has local chapters "DCGs").
- **Black Hat**: More corporate/professional focused.
- **BSides**: Community-driven, cheaper, often local to major cities.
- **GirlScript / Women in CyberSecurity (WiCyS)**: Supportive communities for women.

## ⚠️ Ethics Warning
- **White Hat Only**: Never test systems you don't own or have written permission for.
- **Legal Trouble**: The community shuns "script kiddies" who break laws.
- **Respect Privacy**: Don't share leaked data; discuss the vulnerability, not the victims.

## 🚀 How to Get Value
1. **Start with CTFs**: Capture The Flag competitions teach practical skills.
2. **Write Write-ups**: Document your solutions on a blog; it proves your skills to employers.
3. **Find a Mentor**: Look for seniors in Discord willing to guide your learning path.
4. **Specialize Early**: Try Web AppSec, Network Defense, or Malware Analysis to find your niche.

---
*In security, your reputation is everything. Be helpful, be ethical, and keep learning.*
