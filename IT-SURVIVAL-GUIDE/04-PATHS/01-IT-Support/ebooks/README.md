# IT Support Ebooks

## Free & Legal (Community Regarded)

- **Automate the Boring Stuff with Python** by Al Sweigart
  - Free online.
  - Practical scripting for office tasks.
  - Essential for modern IT automation.

- **Linux Basics for Hackers** by OccupyWallStreet
  - Free PDF available legally.
  - Great intro to Linux CLI.

- **CompTIA A+ Study Guides** (Official Objectives)
  - Not a book, but official exam objectives from CompTIA.
  - Use as a checklist for study topics.
