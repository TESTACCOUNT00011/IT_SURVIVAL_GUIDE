# 🛤️ CHOOSE YOUR PATH

Congratulations! You've completed the **Basics** and **Prerequisites**. Now it's time to choose a specialization.

---

## 🤔 How to Choose?

Don't stress about picking the "perfect" path. Many people:
- Start with one path and switch to another
- Combine skills from multiple paths
- Discover new interests along the way

**The tech world is flexible.** Your first choice doesn't lock you in forever.

---

## 📋 Available Paths

### 1. 💻 Web Development
**What you'll do:** Build websites and web applications

**Good for you if:**
- ✅ You like creating things people can see and use
- ✅ You enjoy both logic and creativity
- ✅ You want relatively quick results (build something in hours/days)

**Career options:**
- Frontend Developer (what users see)
- Backend Developer (server-side logic)
- Full-Stack Developer (both frontend & backend)

📁 **Start here:** `path-01-web-development.md`

---

### 2. 🔧 IT Support / System Administration
**What you'll do:** Help people with tech problems, manage computer systems

**Good for you if:**
- ✅ You like solving puzzles and fixing things
- ✅ You enjoy helping people
- ✅ You're curious about how computers and networks work

**Career options:**
- IT Support Specialist
- System Administrator
- Network Administrator
- IT Manager

📁 **Start here:** `path-02-it-support.md`

---

### 3. 📊 Data Analysis
**What you'll do:** Work with data to find insights and help make decisions

**Good for you if:**
- ✅ You like finding patterns in information
- ✅ You enjoy working with numbers and statistics
- ✅ You're curious about "why" things happen

**Career options:**
- Data Analyst
- Business Intelligence Analyst
- Data Scientist (advanced)

📁 **Start here:** `path-03-data-analysis.md`

---

### 4. 🔒 Cybersecurity
**What you'll do:** Protect systems and data from hackers and attacks

**Good for you if:**
- ✅ You think like both a defender and an attacker
- ✅ You're detail-oriented and thorough
- ✅ You want to work in a high-demand, critical field

**Career options:**
- Security Analyst
- Penetration Tester (ethical hacker)
- Security Engineer
- Incident Responder

📁 **Start here:** `path-04-cybersecurity.md`

---

### 5. ☁️ Cloud Computing
**What you'll do:** Work with cloud platforms to build and manage infrastructure

**Good for you if:**
- ✅ You like working with large-scale systems
- ✅ You're interested in modern infrastructure
- ✅ You want to work with cutting-edge technology

**Career options:**
- Cloud Administrator
- Cloud Engineer
- DevOps Engineer
- Site Reliability Engineer (SRE)

📁 **Start here:** `path-05-cloud-computing.md`

## 🎯 What's Next?

1. **Read all path descriptions** above
2. **Pick ONE** that interests you most
3. **Open that path's file** and follow the roadmap
4. **Build projects** as you learn
5. **Create a portfolio** to show employers
6. **Apply for jobs** or freelance work

---

## 💡 Important Reminders

### You Can Change Paths
Starting web development doesn't mean you're locked in. Many people switch paths or combine skills.

### All Paths Overlap
Skills from one path often help in another. Learning is never wasted.

### Focus on Learning, Not Just Job Titles
The tech landscape changes. Strong fundamentals matter more than specific job titles.

### Build Things
Whatever path you choose, **build projects**. Employers want to see what you can do, not just what you know.

---

## ❓ Still Unsure?

Try this:
1. Spend 1-2 days exploring each path's introduction
2. Try a small beginner project from 2-3 paths
3. Notice which one feels most engaging
4. Go with your gut feeling

**There's no wrong choice.** The best path is the one you actually pursue.
