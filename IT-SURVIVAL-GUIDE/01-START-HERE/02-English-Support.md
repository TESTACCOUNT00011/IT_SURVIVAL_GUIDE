# English Support for IT Learners

IT is an English-dominant field. This note provides strategies and AI prompts to overcome language barriers.

## The Reality

- **Documentation**: 95% of high-quality docs are in English.
- **Error Messages**: Always in English.
- **Community**: StackOverflow, Reddit, and Discord are mostly English.
- **Code**: Syntax keywords (if, else, while, function) are English words.

## Strategy 1: Browser Extensions

Install these immediately:
- **Google Translate** or **DeepL**: For full page translation.
- **Dictionary Anywhere**: Double-click any word to define it.

## Strategy 2: AI Translation Prompts

Copy and paste these prompts into your AI chatbot when you encounter difficult text.

### Prompt: Simplify Technical English
```text
I am a beginner in IT and English is not my first language. 
Please rewrite the following text in simple English (CEFR A2 level). 
Explain any technical jargon in parentheses. 
Text: [PASTE TEXT HERE]
```

### Prompt: Bilingual Explanation
```text
I am learning IT concepts. 
Please explain the following concept in simple English AND translate the key points to [YOUR NATIVE LANGUAGE]. 
Concept: [PASTE CONCEPT OR TEXT HERE]
```

### Prompt: Explain Like I'm 12 with Visuals
```text
Explain this technical concept to me like I am 12 years old. 
Use ASCII art or terminal-style graphics to illustrate the dynamics. 
Keep sentences short. 
Concept: [PASTE CONCEPT HERE]
```

## Strategy 3: Vocabulary Building

Focus on these high-frequency IT words first:
- **Verbs**: Run, Execute, Compile, Debug, Deploy, Fetch, Render.
- **Nouns**: Server, Client, Database, API, Framework, Library, Repository.
- **Adjectives**: Deprecated, Legacy, Robust, Scalable, Redundant.

Create flashcards for these words using the Anki templates in `99-RESOURCES/Anki`.

## Strategy 4: Immersion

- Change your phone and computer language to English.
- Watch tech videos with English subtitles.
- Read one technical article daily, even if you only understand 50%.

> [!TIP] Consistency Over Perfection
> Do not wait until your English is "perfect" to start coding. 
> Learn them together. You will pick up the language faster by doing.
