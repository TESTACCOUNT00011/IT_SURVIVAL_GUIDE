# Career Paths: Map of Content

You have learned the basics. Now it is time to specialize. Below are the five main entry-level paths into the tech industry.

## How to Choose
Not sure which one fits? Take the [[how-to-choose|Vibe Check Quiz]] first.

## The Five Paths

### 1. [[path-01-web-development|Web Development]]
- **What**: Building websites and web applications.
- **Best for**: Creative people who like instant visual feedback.
- **Difficulty**: Medium.
- **Job Market**: Very High demand.

### 2. [[path-02-it-support|IT Support & SysAdmin]]
- **What**: Fixing hardware/software issues, managing networks.
- **Best for**: Problem solvers who like helping people directly.
- **Difficulty**: Low to Medium.
- **Job Market**: Stable demand everywhere.

### 3. [[path-03-data-analysis|Data Analysis]]
- **What**: Turning raw data into business insights.
- **Best for**: Detail-oriented people who like patterns and statistics.
- **Difficulty**: Medium to High (Math heavy).
- **Job Market**: Growing rapidly.

### 4. [[path-04-cybersecurity|Cybersecurity]]
- **What**: Protecting systems from hackers and attacks.
- **Best for**: Curious minds who like puzzles and breaking things (ethically).
- **Difficulty**: High (Requires broad knowledge).
- **Job Market**: Extremely High demand, low supply of workers.

### 5. [[path-05-cloud-computing|Cloud Computing]]
- **What**: Managing servers and infrastructure on AWS/Azure/GCP.
- **Best for**: People interested in how the internet scales.
- **Difficulty**: High.
- **Job Market**: Very High demand.

> [!NOTE] Fail Forward Philosophy
> You can switch paths later. Many professionals start in IT Support, then move to Cloud or Security. Your first job does not define your entire career.

## Next Steps
1. Read the details of 2-3 paths that interest you.
2. Try the "Day 1 Challenge" listed in each path.
3. Commit to one path for 3 months before switching.
