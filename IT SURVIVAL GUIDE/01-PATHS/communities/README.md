## Community Etiquette

Before joining any Discord, Reddit, or Slack:

1. **Read the Rules**: Every community has pinned rules. Read them first.
2. **Search Before Asking**: Your question has likely been answered before.
3. **Show Your Work**: When asking for help, share what you tried and what error you got.
4. **Be Respectful**: Everyone was a beginner once. Do not mock others.
5. **Give Back**: Once you learn something, help the next person.

> [!WARNING] Avoid Scams
> Legitimate communities will NEVER ask for your password, private keys, or payment in DMs. Report anyone who does immediately.
