# 📝 Template Library

A collection of ready-to-use templates for your IT learning journey. Copy these templates into your daily notes or project folders.

## 🗓️ Daily & Routine Templates

### [[Daily-Log|Daily Log Template]]
Track your daily activities, learnings, and reflections.
- **Use for**: Daily journaling, tracking progress, noting challenges
- **Location**: `Daily-Log.md`

### [[Study-Session|Study Session Template]]
Structure your focused study time effectively.
- **Use for**: Pomodoro sessions, topic deep-dives, practice exercises

## 🎯 Project & Goal Templates

### [[Project-Tracker|Project Tracker Template]]
Manage your learning projects from start to finish.
- **Use for**: Portfolio projects, certification prep, skill-building initiatives
- **Includes**: Goals, milestones, resources, blockers, completion criteria

### [[Learning-Summary|Learning Summary Template]]
Consolidate what you've learned after completing a module or topic.
- **Use for**: End-of-week reviews, module completions, concept mastery
- **Includes**: Key takeaways, questions remaining, next steps

### [[Goal-Setter|Goal Setting Template]]
Define and track your short-term and long-term IT career goals.
- **Use for**: Monthly planning, quarterly reviews, certification timelines

## 💼 Career & Interview Templates

### [[Interview-Prep|Interview Preparation Template]]
Prepare systematically for technical interviews.
- **Use for**: Mock interviews, question practice, company research
- **Includes**: Common questions, STAR method examples, technical concepts to review

### [[Meeting-Notes|Meeting Notes Template]]
Take structured notes during meetings, study groups, or mentoring sessions.
- **Use for**: Study group sessions, mentor meetings, networking calls
- **Includes**: Attendees, agenda, action items, follow-ups

### [[Resume-Build|Resume Building Template]]
Structure your IT resume with industry-standard sections.
- **Use for**: Entry-level resumes, internship applications, career transitions

## 🐛 Problem-Solving Templates

### [[Troubleshooting-Log|Troubleshooting Log Template]]
Document technical problems and their solutions systematically.
- **Use for**: Debugging code, fixing system issues, exam preparation
- **Includes**: Problem description, steps taken, solution, lessons learned

### [[Code-Snippet|Code Snippet Template]]
Store and annotate useful code snippets for future reference.
- **Use for**: Reusable functions, configuration examples, quick references

---

## 🚀 How to Use Templates

1. **Copy the template** you need from the files above
2. **Paste it** into your daily note or a new note
3. **Fill in the sections** relevant to your current task
4. **Link related notes** using `[[ ]]` syntax
5. **Review regularly** to track progress

## 💡 Pro Tips

- Create a "Templates" folder in your vault and store copies here
- Use Obsidian's **Templater** plugin for automatic insertion
- Customize templates to fit your personal workflow
- Tag template-based notes with `#template-used` for easy filtering
