# AI Prompt Library

Use these prompts with ChatGPT, Claude, or other AI assistants to accelerate your learning. Copy and paste them directly, then fill in the bracketed sections.

## Category 1: Language & Translation (For Non-Native Speakers)

### Prompt: Translate Technical Jargon
```text
I am learning IT and English is my second language. Please take the following text:
"[PASTE DIFFICULT TEXT OR ERROR MESSAGE HERE]"

1. Explain it in very simple English (CEFR A2 level).
2. Translate the core concept into [YOUR NATIVE LANGUAGE].
3. List the 3 most important technical vocabulary words and define them simply.
```

### Prompt: Bilingual Study Buddy
```text
Act as my bilingual IT tutor. I am studying [TOPIC, e.g., IP Addresses].
- Explain the key points in simple English.
- Provide a summary in [YOUR NATIVE LANGUAGE].
- Quiz me with 3 simple questions in English, then let me answer in my native language if I get stuck.
```

## Category 2: Simplification & Visualization

### Prompt: Explain Like I'm 12 (ELI5) with Visuals
```text
Explain the concept of "[INSERT CONCEPT]" to me like I am 12 years old.
- Use an analogy from real life (e.g., plumbing, traffic, cooking).
- Create an ASCII art diagram or terminal graphic to show how it works visually.
- Avoid jargon where possible.
```

### Prompt: Real-World Analogy Generator
```text
I don't understand [CONCEPT]. Give me 3 different real-world analogies to help me understand it.
Example: "A firewall is like a security guard at a building entrance."
```

## Category 3: Coding & Debugging Help

### Prompt: The Socratic Tutor (Do Not Give Me The Answer)
```text
I am trying to solve this coding problem: "[DESCRIBE PROBLEM]".
Here is my code: [PASTE CODE].
Do not give me the solution. Instead, ask me guiding questions to help me find the bug myself. Explain the concept I am missing.
```

### Prompt: Code Explainer
```text
Explain what this code does line-by-line in simple English:
[PASTE CODE]
```

### Prompt: Generate Practice Exercises
```text
I have just learned about [TOPIC, e.g., Python Lists].
Generate 5 practice exercises for me, ranging from very easy to medium difficulty.
Provide the solutions separately so I can check my work after trying.
```

## Category 4: Career & Roadmap Advice

### Prompt: Personalized Learning Roadmap
```text
I am a complete beginner who wants to become a [JOB TITLE, e.g., Web Developer].
I have [NUMBER] hours per week to study.
Create a 3-month study plan for me that includes:
1. Specific topics to learn each week.
2. Free resources (videos, articles) for each topic.
3. A small project to build at the end of each month.
```

### Prompt: Resume Reviewer
```text
Here is my resume: [PASTE RESUME].
I am applying for junior [JOB TITLE] roles.
Critique my resume. Tell me what to remove, what to add, and how to phrase my projects to sound more professional.
```

> [!TIP] Best Practices
> - Be specific. The more context you give the AI, the better the answer.
> - Verify answers. AI can hallucinate (make things up). Cross-check code and facts with official documentation.
> - Iterate. If the first answer is too complex, reply with "Make it simpler" or "Give me an example."
