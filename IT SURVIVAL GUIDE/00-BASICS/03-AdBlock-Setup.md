# AdBlock Setup

## Why You Need This
Modern websites are filled with ads, pop-ups, and trackers. These distract you, slow down your connection, and can lead to malicious sites. As a learner, you need a clean reading environment.

## The Standard: uBlock Origin
Do not use "AdBlock Plus" or random extensions. **uBlock Origin** is the industry standard. It is free, open-source, and lightweight.

## Installation Steps

### Chrome / Edge / Brave
1. Open your browser.
2. Go to the **Chrome Web Store**.
3. Search for `uBlock Origin` (Look for the purple shield icon by Raymond Hill).
4. Click **Add to Browser** -> **Add Extension**.
5. You will see a red shield icon appear in your toolbar. It should turn purple when active.

### Firefox
1. Open Firefox.
2. Press `Ctrl+Shift+A` to open Add-ons.
3. Search `uBlock Origin`.
4. Click **Add to Firefox**.

## Configuration
Default settings are usually fine. However:
- Click the shield icon.
- Ensure the big power button is **ON**.
- If a site looks broken, click the power button to temporarily disable it for that site only.

> [!WARNING] Fake Extensions
> Many adblockers steal data. Only install **uBlock Origin** by **Raymond Hill** (github.com/gorhill). If the author name is missing, do not install.
