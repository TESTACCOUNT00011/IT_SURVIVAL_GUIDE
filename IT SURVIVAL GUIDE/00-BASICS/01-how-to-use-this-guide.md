# How to Use This Guide

This guide is a **Knowledge Base**, not a textbook. You do not need to read it linearly.

## The Philosophy
- **Fail Forward**: There are no locked doors. If you want to try an advanced topic, go ahead. If you fail, you have learned what you need to study next.
- **Active Learning**: Do not just read. Type the commands. Break things. Fix them.
- **Use Your Tools**: Use the [[00-PROMPTS/README|AI Prompts]] when you get stuck. Use [[99-Exports/Anki/README|Flashcards]] to memorize terms.

## Recommended Flow for Absolute Beginners

1.  **Read the Warning**: Start with [[00-WARNING-English-Bottleneck]].
2.  **Setup Your Environment**: Go to [[00-BASICS/04-adblock]] and [[00-TOOLBELT/README]].
3.  **Learn to Type**: Spend 1 week on [[00-BASICS/03-typing]]. It pays off forever.
4.  **Pick a Path**: Visit [[01-PATHS/how-to-choose]] to see what interests you.
5.  **Start Building**: Follow the "Phase 1" section of your chosen path.

## Navigation Tips
- **Wiki Links**: Click on links like `[[This]]` to jump between notes.
- **Tags**: Look at the top of each note for tags like `#basics` or `#security`.
- **Search**: Use Obsidian's search (Ctrl/Cmd + O) to find concepts quickly.
