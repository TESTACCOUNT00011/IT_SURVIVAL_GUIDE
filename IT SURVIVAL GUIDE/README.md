# IT Survival Guide

Welcome to your comprehensive roadmap for entering the technology industry. This guide is designed for absolute beginners with zero prior experience. It assumes no coding knowledge, no typing speed, and no technical background.

## How to Use This Guide

This is not a book to be read cover-to-cover. It is a **knowledge base**.
1.  **Start at the Basics**: If you are unsure where to begin, go to [[00-BASICS/01-how-to-use-this-guide]].
2.  **Follow Your Interest**: Once comfortable, explore the [[01-PATHS/README|Career Paths]].
3.  **Use the Tools**: Leverage the [[00-PROMPTS/README|AI Prompts]] and [[99-Exports/Anki/README|Flashcards]] to accelerate learning.

## Critical Warning: The English Bottleneck

> [!WARNING] Important: Language Barrier
> The majority of high-quality technical resources, documentation, error messages, and community support are in **English**.
> If English is not your first language, it will become your primary bottleneck.
> **Action Required**: Read [[00-WARNING-English-Bottleneck]] immediately before proceeding.
